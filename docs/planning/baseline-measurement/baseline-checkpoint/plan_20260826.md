## Edge cases & error handling

- Empty adapter set is the *point*, not an error — but only under `untrained: true`. Every
  other empty/contradictory shape is a named `CheckpointUnverified`.
- The provenance digest excludes the provenance itself (existing design, `sft.py:523-528`);
  an untrained checkpoint's integrity seal is the committed artifact (aspect 3), stated in
  the spec's open questions — do not invent a new digest scheme here.
- Hand-edited `untrained: true` on a trained checkpoint: not caught by the file digest (by
  design); out of scope for this aspect (the seal is aspect 3). Do not widen
  `_digest_of`'s inputs.
