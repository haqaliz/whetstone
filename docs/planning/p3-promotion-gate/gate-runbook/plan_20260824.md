## Testing strategy

- The guard suite is the test: it pins the sheet to the shipped parser, constants by identity,
  absolute paths, and the documented homes — watched failing against the stub first.
- The write-up adds no test surface beyond what the existing guards assert (test_docs.py, the
  one-home guard).

## Environment, determinism & locality

- The sheet is operator-executed post-merge; this unit runs no GPU pass.
- Every path the sheet names is absolute; the primary's machine-level roots (`tasks/local`,
  `weights`) are referenced by path, never copied.

## Edge cases

- A refusal exit 2 in the gate names its cause — the sheet maps each refusal to its action.
- An `UNVERIFIED` exit 3 is a published outcome, not a rerun loop — the sheet says so verbatim
  (the roadmap's "the fix is a more reliable sandbox, never a looser gate").
- The killed-gate restart: the sheet states the idempotence/refusal behaviour of the promotion
  record writer, whichever it is.
