## Edge cases (from the spec)

- `bool`-typed counts are rejected at the door (aspect 3); the writer renders what it is
  given — no validation of its own beyond the dataclass's types.
- The UNVERIFIED document must contain no string of the form `+N of M held-out` — the test
  greps for the headline shape, not only for `\d+ of \d+`.
- The rejected-attempt disclosure must not read as a win: the candidate's counts are labeled
  "rejected attempt", never "final".
