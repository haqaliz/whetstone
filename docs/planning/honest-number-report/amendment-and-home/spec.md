# Spec — amendment-and-home (aspect 4 of honest-number-report)

## Problem slice

The delta/final series needs a home and a pre-registration disclosure, both before any figure
exists. `CLAUDE.md` pre-commits the amendment: the loop's generation contract (seeded
categorical sampler, `sampling.K = 8`) differs from every published one (greedy), and "the
amendment belongs to whichever later unit first publishes a figure measured under it" — this
unit is that later unit. The one-home guard must move a sixth time on the argued series basis,
and the declaration-only artifacts must be committed first, writer-generated, never hand-typed.

## In-scope

- **The § 10.9 Type 2 amendment** (`PREREGISTRATION.md` § 10, before any figure): discloses
  the final side's generation contract — the loop's seeded categorical sampler — as the
  contract the final-side figures are measured under; declares `reports/honest-number/` the
  only home of the delta/final series' figures; names the existing homes non-comparable in
  the § 10.6 shape ("a figure measured under a changed contract is not comparable to one
  measured under another"); the "At the time of this amendment no count has been measured
  under the report, and none is claimed here" sentence; introduces no success threshold;
  rewords nothing above § 10; adds no proportion in any spelling; nothing amended silently.
- **The one-home guard's sixth move:** both lock-step 15-artifact lists grow by exactly
  three lines (`reports/honest-number/{report.md,report.json,cost.json}`) —
  tests/bakeoff/test_report.py:1941-1957 and tests/bakeoff/test_transcript_locality.py:134-150 —
  each with the argument restated in its docstring (the six-move history: bake-off ran → D6 →
  changed task set → changed candidate → changed series → **the delta/final series under the
  loop's contract**); `_ALL_HOMES` (test_report.py:1322-1327) gains the name; the
  figures-level disjointness scans cover the new home; the planted-overlap control
  (`test_the_one_home_guard_catches_a_planted_artifact`) is re-proven able to fail.
- **The committed declaration-only artifacts:** `reports/honest-number/` three artifacts,
  generated by the writer's declaration state (aspect 2), holding no `N of M` in any
  spelling — committed in this aspect, before any figure.
- **The baseline-home exception is argued, not silent:** the report legitimately renders the
  baseline's figures (the § 4 shape requires it); the exception is loader-by-identity only —
  the door's byte-equality assertion (aspect 3) is the mechanism; the docstrings state it.

## Acceptance criteria

1. `PREREGISTRATION.md` § 10.9 is committed with the row in the amendment log, dated
   2026-08-27, Type 2, the disclosure sentences above verbatim-shaped, and no proportion in
   any spelling (the `test_docs.py` no-figure guards pass).
2. The guard lists hold exactly 18 artifacts (six homes × three), in both lock-step copies,
   with the argument in both docstrings; a silent extension (planted artifact) still fails.
3. `_ALL_HOMES` gains `honest-number`; the disjointness scans cover it; the planted-overlap
   control still catches a planted figure.
4. The committed `reports/honest-number/` artifacts are writer-generated (regeneration is
   byte-identical), hold the declaration sentence, and hold no `\d+ of \d+` figure in any
   spelling.
5. `tests/test_docs.py` (no report without its pre-registration, no placeholder, no figure)
   passes unchanged.
6. The amendment's "no count is claimed here" sentence is asserted in a test (the § 10.6
   pattern).
